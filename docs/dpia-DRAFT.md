# Data protection impact assessment (DRAFT)

> Draft for the Controller (the club) and a lawyer to complete. Not legal advice. Follows the ICO's DPIA steps. Open items are marked **open**. A DPIA is likely needed because the data is health data about people in a working relationship, collected systematically.

## 1. What the processing is

Players answer a short daily check-in. Written rules read the answers and the last 14 days and may propose a small cut to that day's session. Staff approve or reject. Optional wearable data (Polar built; others not connected) can supply sleep. Data stored in London. Full data list and recipients: `PRIVACY-DRAFT.md`.

## 2. Why it is needed, and whether it is proportionate

- **Purpose:** reduce the chance a tired or sore player is given a load that does not suit them that day, and save staff time on large squads.
- **Is less data possible?** The check-in is five quick answers. Wearable data is optional. Free-text notes are optional and 500 characters at most. The staff email carries no health detail.
- **Evidence it works:** none yet. The rules are drafts. A sports scientist has not reviewed them (**open**). Do not describe them as validated.
- **Player choice:** players agree before the first check-in and can delete their data. In an employment setting consent may not be free (**open**: lawyer, lawful basis and Article 9 condition).

## 4. Risks and what reduces them

| Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Mitigations in place | Remaining |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health data seen by someone who should not see it (another club, another player) | Low | High | Per-club separation, server-only database access, private one-phone links | No audit log; no two-factor |
| Staff or club use the data against a player (selection, contract) | Medium | High | Purpose stated in the notice; players see what is shared | Needs club policy and an agreement not to use it that way (**open**) |
| Players feel pressured to check in or to report well | Medium | Medium | Players can decline; pain and illness never auto-edit; data is flagged to staff, not scored | Club culture; wording to staff |
| A rule gives a poor suggestion | Medium | Medium | Staff approve every change; hard limits (no increases, at most 25% cut); each proposal names its rule | Rules unreviewed (**open**) |
| Wrong data from a wearable | Medium | Low | Player confirms or corrects device sleep; compared with the player's own history | Different brands measure differently |
| Data kept longer than needed | High today | Medium | Players and staff can delete | No automatic retention (**open**: pick a period and build it) |
| Breach at a provider, or transfer outside the UK | Low | High | London database; minimal staff email | Provider terms unconfirmed (**open**) |
| Account takeover (staff) | Medium | High | Hashed passwords; reset tokens single-use and short-lived | No two-factor; sessions not revoked on reset |
| Players under 18 | Low | High | Adults only in the pilot | Needs parental consent and safeguarding before any academy use |
