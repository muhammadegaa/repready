**Who this is for:** players aged 18 and over at a club that uses RepReady.

## Who is responsible for your data

Your club ([club name]) decides why your check-in answers are used. They are the "controller". RepReady ([company name, address]) handles the data for your club and only on their instructions. You can contact either:

- Your club: [club contact]
- RepReady: [email]

## What we collect, and why

When you check in: hours slept, soreness (0 to 10), stress (0 to 10), whether you can train today, and an optional note. After training: how hard the session felt (1 to 10). If you connect a watch or ring: sleep hours, heart-rate variability and lowest night-time heart rate. Your name and shirt number are entered by you or your club.

This is health information. We use it for one purpose: to help your club's staff adjust your training session. We do not use it for selection, contracts or anything else, and we do not sell it.

## How it is used

Fixed, written rules read your numbers. They are not an AI service, and no outside AI service receives your answers. A rule can suggest a small change to your session, such as one set fewer. Pain, illness or "can't train" answers are flagged to staff and never change your session on their own. A member of staff approves or rejects each suggestion, or has told the system to apply routine small reductions for them, which they can take back. You only see the session your staff have approved.

## Who sees it

- Your club's staff who use RepReady.
- Our service providers: Google (database, London), Vercel (hosting), Resend (staff emails; players are named in the staff morning email but their scores and notes are not).
- Polar, only if you choose to connect a Polar device. You can disconnect at any time from your page.

## Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw consent. You can delete all your own data yourself with the "Delete my data" button on your RepReady page. You can also ask your club or us. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters to you, we will tell you before your next check-in.
