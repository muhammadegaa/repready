# Who uses RepReady, what their day is like, and what each expects (DRAFT)

Written 2026-10-03 to decide what to build next. It is a working hypothesis from the JTBD boards and from how clubs run, not research. The pilot club replaces guesses with facts.

## The people

| Person | Their day in one line | What they want to optimise | What they will not tolerate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S&C coach** (the buyer and daily user) | In at 7, scans who is ready, runs two or three gym groups, writes next week's program in the evening, speaks to the head coach and physio | Fewer surprises: no player injured by a session that should have been trimmed; no time lost chasing answers; one honest picture of the squad | A tool that adds admin, makes them re-type what they already have, or changes a player's session without them |
| **Sports scientist / performance analyst** (sometimes the same person) | Collects GPS, wellness and load; reports to the staff; checks what is working | Trends over weeks, not one morning; load against response; evidence that a rule helps | Black-box decisions, rules they cannot inspect or change |
| **Physio / medical** (reads, rarely types) | Knows who is injured, rehabbing, or doubtful for the weekend | To hear about pain early, in the player's words, and to see who is back and on what restrictions | Health notes buried in a feed; automatic edits that override their clearance |
| **Head coach** (not a user yet) | Picks the team; wants a yes or no on availability before selection | One screen: who is available, who is doubtful, who is carrying load | Detail. They want the verdict and the reason in a line |
| **Player** (18+) | Wakes up, checks a phone, wants the answer to "what do I do today" | To be believed, to be asked little, to see their session clearly, to know the coach saw their note | Long forms, surveillance, being told off for a bad night |
| **Club admin / owner** (pays) | Cares that it is used and that data is safe | Evidence it is used: answer rate, decisions made. Safe, deletable data | Surprise bills, a tool the staff stop opening |

## What each one needs to see, at a glance

1. **Squad: one picture.** Every player down the side, the last two weeks across the top. Each cell is that player's day against *their own usual*, with matches marked, minutes played, flags and plan changes on it. In ten seconds the coach sees who is trending down, who has gone quiet, who has played a lot, who is back from injury, and whether the whole squad dipped after a heavy week. The head coach and physio read the same picture. This is the new Squad map.
2. **Player: one page.** The same story for one person: their usual band, today against it, minutes, pain notes, standing plan changes with review dates, and what the rules suggested and what the coach did.
3. **Morning: one list.** Three lines: who needs me, who has not answered, what changed overnight. Everything else is one click away.
4. **Week: one draft.** The coming week proposed from last week and the fixture list, to correct rather than to write.
5. **Club: one number.** Answer rate and decisions made (Results).

## The coach's week, and who does what

| Moment | Coach does today | Agent should do | Built? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday evening | Re-pastes next week's program | Draft next week from last week and the fixtures; coach fixes in words | Not yet |
| Fixtures change | Types dates | Keep them in sync with the club calendar | Calendar link: building now |
| Morning | Reads each card | Summarise who needs them; chase silent players | Summary: partly. Chasing: needs a channel decision |
| Suggestion to decide | Approve or keep, one at a time | Offer to handle routine ones inside limits, if the coach opts in | Needs a decision |
| Day after a match | Remembers to log minutes | Ask who played, once, on that day | Building now |
| Player reports pain | Follows up outside the app | Propose a dated return note and a check-back | Not yet |
| Standing change for a player | Fills a form | Say it in words, confirm a draft | Not yet (privacy decision for names) |
| Any day | Opens several pages to find out how the squad is | Look at one map | Building now |

## What would make this a real product, not a form
- Anything the coach already has (calendar, spreadsheet, message) goes in as it is.
- The agent notices and asks; the coach decides.
- Every number is against that player's own normal, not a league table.
- Hard limits stay in code: no load above plan, no cut over a quarter, pain stops automation.
