# RepReady pilot offer (DRAFT)

**For:** the strength and conditioning coach of a football club. **Read time:** two minutes.

## What it does
Each morning your players answer a few questions on their phone (sleep, soreness, mood, anything that hurts). RepReady compares that with today's session and each player's own usual range, and proposes **at most one change** to that player's session, such as one fewer set or a lighter load. **You approve or keep the plan.** Nothing changes for a player until you say so. It never raises a load above your plan and never cuts more than a quarter.

## What you do
1. Sign up and tell it your program: paste it as you wrote it, or upload your spreadsheet. It shows you what it read; you correct it in plain words and press Use this program.
2. Add your squad the same way: paste a list or upload a spreadsheet. Players open a link on their phone, agree to the terms, and check in.
3. Each morning open Today. Flags and suggestions are there; approve or keep.

Groups (Starters, Reserves) get their own version of a session. One player can have a standing change to their plan (a lighter squat for a knee, for example) which the rules respect.

## What it is not
Not a medical tool and not a diagnosis. A pain flag is shown to you with no automatic change. It does not replace your judgement.

## Your players' data
Held in the UK (London). Players must be 18 or over and agree on their own phone before anything is stored. Their answers are seen by club staff, not by anyone else. The rules run in our own code. An outside assistant reads **only the program text you paste and the sentences you type into the Ask bar**, with player names replaced by codes, and never player answers. You can delete a player and their data at any time. See the privacy notice for the full list of services we use.

## What we ask of the pilot club
- A squad of 15 or more, and you checking in with the app most mornings for four weeks.
- A short call at the end of week two and week four.
- Honest feedback on what was wrong, including suggestions you would not have made.

## How we will judge it together
The Results page shows two numbers: the share of players who check in, and how often you act on a suggestion. We suggest the pilot works if most players answer most mornings, and you find the suggestions worth reading.
